Graduate students demonstrate common Core competence through **AI-off diagnostics** on every unit checkpoint (remediating through the Core where needed), then follow one or more specializations. All end in a research capstone at workshop-paper standard with an oral defense.

| Specialization | Emphasis | Atlas regions |
|---|---|---|
| **Models and learning algorithms** | representation, architectures, objectives, generative learning, adaptation, reasoning, post-training | mechanisms, architectures, paradigms |
| **Computational learning systems** | efficient operators, distributed training, low precision, inference, deployment, energy and latency budgets | systems, methods |
| **Scientific ML and autonomous systems** | neural operators, inverse problems, physics-informed methods, multimodal time series, control, world models, agents | paradigms, methods |

**Exit standard.** A graduate can propose a consequential question, state an evaluable hypothesis, design a discriminating experiment, use AI to execute it at a scale they could not reach alone, and defend precisely what the resulting evidence does not establish.
